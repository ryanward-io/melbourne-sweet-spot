    About and data

    I built this as a personal house-hunting tool. Distances and listings can be out of date, and being close to something says nothing about how good it is, so check anything that matters.

    Walking distances follow OpenStreetMap paths on a 50 m grid, rounded to 20 m and capped at 5 km. Spot checks against 60 OSRM walking routes differed by about 26 m (median).

    Map and places © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), as of 4 November 2026. Places also from Overture Maps Foundation (October 2026 release).

    From the State of Victoria, under CC BY 4.0: planning zones, suburbs and open space (Vicmap); school locations and 2027 zones (Department of Education); train and tram stops (Public Transport Victoria via DataVic); median rents by rental area for the year to June 2026 (Homes Victoria); and median sale prices for 2025 and the March quarter 2026 (Valuer-General Victoria).

    High school rankings are Better Education’s 2025 list (bettereducation.com.au), their own score rather than a VCE ranking. Gyms were checked against each chain’s or gym’s own website on 6 November 2026.
